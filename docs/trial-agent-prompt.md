# Prompt for an agent making a trial-style Short

Copy everything below the line into a new session, then paste the transcript where it says so.

---

Make a vertical YouTube Short of Dr. David E. Arredondo teaching from the Tao Te Ching (Thomas Cleary
translation), cut from one of our long-form videos. The repo is `lexowalker/YouTube-Shorts`. The moodboard is in
`moodboard/` on the branch `claude/compassionate-brown-zu6btm`.

## The transcript

[PASTE THE TIMESTAMPED TRANSCRIPT HERE]

If I give you teachings instead of a finished transcript, first suggest transcript excerpts from the video that
embody each teaching, in the order they appear. I'll finalise them before you make the video.
- Leave out unnecessary elements (ums, ahs, gaps, etc.).
- If an excerpt quotes the chapter's Tao Te Ching text (e.g. a couplet), the video starts with that excerpt.
- Aim for 2:00–3:00 in total.

## The style

Each video contains:

1. **Talking-head sections:** the speaker, Dr. Arredondo, speaking as in the original video.
2. **Images,** with very slight zoom or pan effects.
3. **Animated collages.**
4. **Subtitles** timed and keyed to the audio.
5. **An end card.**

**Talking head.** Crop, frame and centre every talking-head section the same way, so all the head scenes are
consistent. The original colour of the videos is washed out after uploading to the Internet Archive, so restore
the natural colours.

**Images.** Follow the moodboard: ancient East Asian iconography, statuary, woodblock prints, scrolls, etc. The
moodboard sets the style of the imagery. Images can come from anywhere, but they must be high quality and follow
the moodboard's aesthetic. If you use a pan or zoom, keep it very light and tasteful. Too much, or too fast, is
disorienting.

**The most important aspect of the imagery: it is keyed to the transcript and audio.**
- Talking about the simplicity and naturalness of birds → ancient East Asian woodblock prints of birds.
- Disarray in a populace → a bustling city centre in disarray.
- Inflaming passions → an angry dragon or an angry yokai.
- Abiding by the Way, like the wind → leaves or branches in the wind.

The imagery and the audio go hand in hand and make sense together.

**Collages.** Follow the animated collage style of this video: https://youtu.be/CMqw9usH8-8. Ours are simpler
and smaller, since they're in a portrait Short and last only 5–15 s at a time. What I like about the reference:
- A balanced use of black-and-white and colour.
- Simple animated movements of the pieces.
- The collage being built up: only a background and a couple of images at first, then a couple more, then a
  couple more.

Collages follow the same points as the imagery (keyed to the words), but with more freedom in the images used.
They needn't follow the moodboard tightly.

**Subtitles.** Same placement, font and colouring as the most recent teaching Short on the `main` branch (not
the reading Shorts, which have a different style).

**End card.** The same end card as the Shorts on the `main` branch.

**Timing.** Each scene lasts a few seconds or more. No quick, disorienting cuts.

## Caveats from the first attempt

- **The washed-out colour:** the 4K source files are HDR (HLG). That's why they look washed out when decoded as
  ordinary video. Restore the colour with a proper HDR-to-SDR conversion rather than boosting saturation.
- **Subtitles:** keep collage pieces and important parts of images out of the area where the subtitles sit.
- **Faces:** don't let collage pieces cover faces (a statue's or a figure's).
- **Cut-outs:** a cut-out with holes punched through it looks broken. Use a torn scrap of the picture instead.
- **When something isn't covered:** if something isn't covered here, ask me rather than guessing.
