## 2. The teaching

## 5. Notes on choices
- **Imagery:** the hook stays on his face. The Moon follows Pluto as "a rock floating in the sky" whose name is ours, not its own. Bierstadt's California and western mountains carry the Mexico lines. For the money line, an old copper plate coin stands in for the bill, as money that exists because people agree it does. Hokusai's Red Fuji closes on "the reality is still there": whatever we call it, the mountain stands.
- **Passed over:** Goryeo bronze seals and a Kushan gold coin (grey museum backdrops under the captions), and Kenyon Cox's *Design for United States Currency* (literal, but from 1912, and the house style takes no 20th-century work).
- **Words:** large-v3 and small.en hear "the name" twice in "You can have a reality without having the name", and the captions follow them; medium.en and the QA pass hear "a name", and QA hears "could". The line "But no matter what the name is, the reality is still there" was misheard by medium.en and is timed from a narrow large-v3 pass and the envelope.
- **Head share:** 54%. The Fuji crop keeps the peak, so the closing captions sit on the pink slope; they stay readable.
- **Listen once:** I can't hear the audio. Please check the joins at 0:25.2 (into "The country of Mexico"), 0:51.5 (into "So, individually") and 1:03.4 (the trimmed hesitation in "But no… matter").
