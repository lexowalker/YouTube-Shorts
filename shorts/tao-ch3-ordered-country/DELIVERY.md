## 1. Title
An Ordered Government, an Ordered Country (recommended)

## 2. The teaching
"Not exalting cleverness causes the people not to contend." Cleary's notes: cleverness here means the cunning and craft sought by contending lords. Su Che's reading: sage rulers do employ the intelligent, they just don't exalt them; rare goods stay in plain view and theft does not rise. So an ordered government wants an ordered country, where a natural sanity arises if it isn't interfered with. We will need to rebuild that, starting with how we raise our children, with sane people willing to work for the commonweal.

## 3. Transcript as heard
“Not exalting cleverness causes the people not to contend.” “Here, cleverness is used in the sense of cunning and craft, such as sought by contending lords hoping to learn the ways of aggrandizing themselves and enlarging their holdings.” Sound familiar? “Taoist philosophers did not believe that it was a good sign for society when cunning intellectuals were competing for the attention of the rich and powerful.” “Su Che, who is a statesman as well as a poet and mystic, presents another view of this passage and the following passage that it introduces.” “It is not that sage rulers don't employ the intelligent, only that they don't exalt them. They don't throw away goods that are hard to find, they just don't put high prices on them. They don't get rid of whatever is desirable, they just don't see it.” They just don't pay attention to it. “Thus, the intelligent are employed, but the people don't compete. Rare goods and desirable things are after all in plain view, but theft, robbery, and malicious disturbance do not rise.” So, if you have an ordered government, what they would want to create is an ordered country. If you have an ordered country, there's a natural sanity that if you don't interfere with it, it just arises. And people don't go breaking into each other's cars and houses and killing them and robbing them and all that other stuff. There's a general sense that, hey, that's not how we do things around here. And it's very clear that even if there is not an economic or a political collapse, even if there's not geopolitical catastrophe, we're going to need to rebuild this country. I've been in child welfare and juvenile justice for a very large chunk of my life, probably half of it. And things have just gone completely off the rails. And so we can't do that with our own children, because if we do, what we're going to end up with is, those are the people who are going to be taking care of us, taking care of you. So you've got to have within your cohort sane people that are willing to work for the good of the people, the commonweal. Or it's going to come back and bite you sooner or later.
