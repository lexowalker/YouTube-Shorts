## 5. Notes on choices
- **Imagery:** the Thinker for a lifetime of seeing, hearing and thinking (the "first attention", the intellect at work); Piranesi's Colosseum for "the largest stadium"; star deities descending from the night sky for "the universe". The handscroll is used twice on purpose: a close crop on a tiny footpath with travelers for "a little pathway by a major road", then a slow pan across its endless mountains for "the real is… larger than the universe", so the small path and the vast scroll are literally the same picture.
- **Passed over:** a silk of dragons chasing flaming pearls for "a marble" (the pearls are lost in the pattern), and Hiroshige's Kiso Road snow print for the path (pale paper).
- **Captions on the hook:** he cups his hands to show "small" right behind the captions at 0:02–0:05; they stay readable, and the gesture is worth keeping.
- **Head share:** 40%. The Colosseum holds 12.1 s, at the house limit.
- **Listen once:** I can't hear the audio. Please check the first join at 0:05.2–0:05.6 (the inserted room tone and the start of "If we take"), and 0:14.1 ("ping pong ball, | This is how many books").
