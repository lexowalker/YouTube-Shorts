## 1. Hook title
Be Careful What You Call Yours

## 2. The teaching
Whatever you call yours becomes part of you: call a car "you" and its dents hurt you; call a tree "me", or the whole living planet yours, and it becomes part of you, so be careful what you call yours (Tao Te Ching, Chapter 4, "assimilates to the world", Thomas Cleary translation).

## 5. Notes on the choices
- **Why this teaching.** It's the most quotable, practical idea in the video after "unimpeded harmony", and it doesn't overlap the first Short. It's David's own reading of "assimilates to the world": what you identify with becomes you.
- **Shape.** The hook states the principle. The tree and planet passage shows that the living part of things is a thin outer mantle that we belong to. The car story makes the principle personal and a little funny. The tree and planet return as the hopeful side, and the closing line is its own conclusion.
- **Pictures.** A car can't appear in a classical ink style, so Ren Renfa's groomed horse stands in as the "beautiful car" of its day, with a slow pan along the handscroll ending on its bridled head. The car story itself stays on David's face, because his delivery carries the humor.
- **Caption accuracy.** Three passes (medium.en, a second medium.en and large-v3) were compared wherever they disagreed. The corrections are recorded in `short.json` as `word_overrides` with the reason for each. "Oh, but my car" comes from large-v3 at high confidence. "In some manner of form" stayed uncertain, so it's in the audio but not on screen.
- **Image swap.** Dong Qichang's *Mt. Qingbian* was replaced by Ma Lin's *Flying Geese* because its pale paper made the caption unreadable.
- **Sound.** The original recording, cut in pauses with 12 ms fades and de-clicked only between words, with one static gain to a −1 dBTP peak. Result: −19.5 LUFS integrated.
- **Thumbnails.** `thumbnails/thumbnail_face.jpg` uses the frame you picked (0:18.8 of the source, a quiet pause looking into the camera). `thumbnail_book_cover.jpg` is *The Essential Tao* cover (Amazon's 306×500 scan, so it's soft at full size).
