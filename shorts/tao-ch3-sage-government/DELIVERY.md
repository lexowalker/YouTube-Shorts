## 1. Recommended title
What If Sages Ran the Government?

## 2. The teaching
"The government of sages empties the mind and fills the middle, weakens the ambition and strengthens the bones, always keeping the people innocent and passionless." Sages in government would not be full of cleverness, psyops and fear; that behaviour hurts a government's own people. The whole function of government is an environment where people can flourish. Act without contrivance: be straightforward, direct.

## 3. Transcript as heard
“Therefore the government of sages empties the mind and fills the middle, weakens the ambition and strengthens the bones, always keeping the people innocent and passionless.” Can you imagine if we had sages running the government instead of the clown show that we have of these politicians? They wouldn't be all full of cleverness. They wouldn't be all full of, “Oh, we're going to use this psyop. Oh, we're going to use this manipulation of the voter. Oh, we're going to scare them with this. We're going to entice them with this. We're going to scare the hell out of them that the opposing party is going to destroy the country.” All of this non-sage-like behavior, because when you do that, you hurt your own people. And that's what's happening. We've hurt our own people. Our government is hurting us by incessant lying and psychological operations that are designed to inculcate fear or hatred or worse. It “weakens the ambition and strengthens the bones.” If your country is just full of ambitious people, there's nobody to run anything because everybody's too busy trying to figure out a way to get ahead. So ideally, you'd want government where people were not ambitious. They didn't go in there to compete with each other as to who could get the highest net worth or position. “Always keeping the people innocent and passionless,” which is kind of the opposite of what we're doing now. What we're doing now is making the people afraid and angry and putting each other at each other's throats, which is ridiculous. You don't want to foment emotion in your people. You want them to be chill. You want them to live their lives. That's the whole function of government, is to create an environment where people can flourish and do their work and contribute and take what they need. So we're a long way from that. “It makes the sophisticated not dare to contrive. Action being without contrivance, nothing is disordered.” So in a situation like this where benevolence actually rules in the land, where there's actually goodwill, where there's actually integrity and good faith without the extremes, then the sophisticated, clever people, they don't dare to contrive because they know that they're going to get seen through. This is a pillar of Taoism, and that is to act without contrivance. Just be straightforward, direct.
