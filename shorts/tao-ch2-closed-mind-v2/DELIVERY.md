## 1. Hook title
You Can Be Bigoted on the Right, the Left, or the Center

## 2. The teaching
Cleary's note on Chapter 2: "When it is forgotten that conventional conceptions are conventional conceptions, and they are taken for objective facts that everyone knows and no one questions, then narrow-minded bigotry and blind prejudice can develop unopposed." Bigotry means a closed mind, locked into one way of conceiving things, when our whole way of looking at the world is conditioned by our environment. Anyone who can look more holistically sees more clearly.

## 5. Notes on choices
- **"Everyone knows and no one questions":** a flock going the one way behind its shepherd. The shot pans slowly from the sheep to the shepherd, because the drawing is too wide to show both in one 9:16 crop.
- **Blind prejudice:** kept from v1. It's the only literal picture of the words.
- **A closed mind:** one heavy shut door, held through "not open… locked into one fixed way".
- **Changing attitudes:** autumn on the mountain as a storm clears: the weather and the season both turning.
- **Instagram, TV, TikTok:** a classical stand-in that keeps the meaning, a crowd of faces all turned up to the same spectacle. It comes in on "conditioned… by your environment" and holds through the list, so the crowd is the environment.
- **A slice of the world:** a room that sees the city only through one narrow doorway.
- **Ending:** two men on a high terrace looking out over the whole misty lake.
- **Head share:** 35%, with nine images. No mode runs over 11.8 s.
- **Draft flags:** BRIGHT shows on some head-shot captions (his grey sweater) and TIGHT on the same joins as v1. The head shots and cuts are v1's approved ones, with the one change above.
- **Listen once:** I can't hear the audio. Please check the join at 1:27.3 ("environment, | you know, what you see on Instagram"), the one cut that differs from v1. Also check 1:24.6 ("totally | conditioned").
