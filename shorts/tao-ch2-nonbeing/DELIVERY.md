## 2. The teaching
"So being and nonbeing produce each other." Nonbeing is God, the Nagual, the Tao, the infinite, the nameless: the ground that produces everything that exists. Even science can't say what was there before the Big Bang. Good scientists know they can only describe the world, never get to it (Tao Te Ching, Chapter 2, Thomas Cleary translation).

## 5. Notes on choices
- **Opening:** peaks forming out of empty mist for "being and non-being produce each other", then Juran's mountain rising from stream to sky for "heaven and earth".
- **Names of the nameless:** the endless landscape carries God, the Nagual, the Tao and nature. The real infinite, the Deep Field, comes in on "the infinite" and holds through the pause to "substrate".
- **Everything that exists:** a scroll of the flowers of all four seasons, panning slowly.
- **Big Bang:** Hiroshige's single firework bursting in a dark sky. It's a classical stand-in that keeps the meaning, and it comes in on "Let's use the Big Bang theory".
- **Science:** scholars holding up an armillary sphere, a model of the heavens, which is a description of the world rather than the world itself.
- **Ending:** on his face for "Not all of them do, but the good ones do."
