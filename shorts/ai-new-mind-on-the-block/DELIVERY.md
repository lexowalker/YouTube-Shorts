## 1. Title
Recommended: "There's a New Mind on the Block" (see section 8).

## 2. The teaching
A new mind has arrived that will outstrip us intellectually. It will not threaten our bodies so much as our minds: our idea of ourselves as the dominant, most intelligent thing on the planet. People are already forming relationships with these AIs and can be exploited through them. If you don't understand what you are, you will be a victim of whatever comes up. We are not at the center, and that should give us a little humility.
