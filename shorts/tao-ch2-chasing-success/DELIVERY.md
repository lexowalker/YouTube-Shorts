## 1. Hook title
The More You Chase Success, the Less You See

## 2. The teaching
"All beings work without exception: if they live without possessiveness, act without presumption, and do not dwell on success, then by this very nondwelling success will not leave." Do your work, do your best and keep going. Obsess over success, measured in money and fame, and you can't see clearly. It's like trying to be happy, which is a recipe for misery (Tao Te Ching, Chapter 2, Thomas Cleary translation).
