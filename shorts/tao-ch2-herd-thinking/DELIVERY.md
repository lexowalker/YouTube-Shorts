## 1. Hook title
When Everyone Agrees, Something Is Being Overlooked

## 2. The teaching
"When everyone knows beauty is beauty, this is bad; when everyone knows good is good, this is not good." When everyone agrees that something is good, nobody asks what could be wrong with it, so something gets overlooked. Every coin has two sides, so question what the herd takes for granted (Tao Te Ching, Chapter 2, Thomas Cleary translation).

## 5. Notes on choices
- **Round 3 (the user's process changes):** it opens on the Cleary quote, captioned in quotation marks; captions sit at 66% of the height (above YouTube's overlay) with a stronger halo; the imagery was replaced from scratch with literal matches (the user's photo of a party crowd admiring one woman, a real thalidomide pack, Dutch tulip fields, a herd of sheep, a crowd photographing the Mona Lisa, a line of near-identical beauty contestants); every image is shown whole.
- **Round 2 (the user's notes):**
  - **Colour:** his face looked washed out. The 3/28/24 upload is HDR (HLG, BT.2020), and the pipeline had been reading it as ordinary video. Head shots, drafts and thumbnails are now tone-mapped to SDR BT.709 on decode (`common.sdr_filter`, used automatically for any HDR source), which restores his natural colour.
  - **Coins:** both faces are now whole.
  - **Thalidomide:** the decorated pharmacy jar didn't read as medicine. An open 17th-century apothecary's chest, with its rows of medicine bottles, replaces it.
  - **Ending:** the Laozi image is gone, and he speaks to camera from "So at a personal level" to the end card.
- **The coin:** a single coin, shown face then reverse. The Apollo head comes up on "every coin has two sides", and the lion arrives on "One side may be…", cut on the join.
- **Herd:** sheep packed together on a road for "herd thinking". For "the herd mentality of everyone deciding this is beautiful" I used Homer's crowd, every face turned the same way.
- **Beauty, twice:** a classical Chinese beauty carries the Cleary line. A celebrated Japanese beauty of her day stands in for Miss America, the beauty everyone agreed on.
- **Left on his face:** the thalidomide harm ("fetuses… deformities"). An image there would be either gruesome or vague.
- **Captions:** the 4K close-up puts his beard at 70% of the height, so captions sit at 83%, on his grey sweater. That sweater is lighter than the tan one in earlier videos, so `draft.py` flags it as BRIGHT. Checked full size, the halo keeps every line readable.
- **Head share:** 59%. The closing head run is 18.2 s, longer than the usual 12 s cap, because the user asked for the ending to stay on his face.
- **Listen once:** I can't hear the audio. Please check two places: the join at 0:16.4 ("If everyone knows something is good, | something's being overlooked"), where a 3 s pause was shortened, and the straight cut on his face at 1:34.75 ("basic counsel. | Take this into your life").
