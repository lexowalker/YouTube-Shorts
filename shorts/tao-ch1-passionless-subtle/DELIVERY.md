## 1. Hook title
Why Trying Harder Makes You Miss What Matters

## 2. The teaching
Always passionless, thereby observe the subtle; ever intent, thereby observe the apparent. Passion, anger, anxiety and craving hide the subtle, attention shows us what is in front of us, and we need both (Tao Te Ching, Chapter 1, Thomas Cleary translation).

## 5. Notes on choices
- **Teaching:** the user chose this one of three candidates (the others: "A way can be a guide, not a fixed path" and "Names aren't permanent labels", both left for Discussion 2) and approved the transcript before work began.
- **Hook first:** the only reordering. "Too much effort is counterproductive" comes right after his list of what blocks the subtle, and it states the whole teaching in one line.
- **No title card:** the user had the opening "To see the subtle, be still" removed; the hook line states the teaching on its own.
- **Imagery (recut):** the first cut's images were in the mood of the words but not always of them, and the user asked for each to show what he is saying. Every image now lands on the word that names its subject: mist for "the subtle", a falcon on "Ever intent", a leaping tiger for "vim and vigor", Sesson's storm dragon for anger, anxiety and confusion, lovers for "lust" (replacing the gibbon reaching for the moon), Ma Yuan's scholar watching deer for "pay attention… what's right in front of you", carried on through "hear that bell" (a bronze bell was tried there and dropped at the user's request: one image per idea, not per noun), Hanshan reading his scroll for "study this book", Bodhidharma at his wall for putting it into practice (replacing the monk mending), galloping riders as the stand-in for "run over by a bus", figures gazing at a waterfall for the quiet that sees, the handscroll for "the whole universe", and the moon for "so, so subtle". The passions (tiger, dragon, lovers, the riders) are deliberately turbulent and worldly, set against the quiet mist, water and moon of the Way.
- **Screens and crops:** folding-screen crops stay inside one panel. The egret and Ma Lin's round fan (*Scholar Reclining and Watching Rising Clouds*) were tried for "pay attention" and "observe the subtle nuance", but the egret's pale paper swallowed the captions and in 9:16 Ma Lin's scholar fell under the captions and the rest read as empty, so Sōami replaced it.
- **Crops recentred (second recut):** the falcon is now taken wider (x 0–0.72, softer 1.04× zoom) so the whole bird and its eye sit inside the frame, and the lovers crop is centred on the couple instead of running off their right side.
- **Caption height:** 83% instead of the house 70%. The 4K full-height crop frames him closer, and at 70% the off-white captions sat on his white beard (seen on the first render), so they now sit on his sweater.
- **Head share:** 40%, under the usual 50–60%, because the teaching is unusually concrete and imageable; no mode runs longer than 9.8 s, and no shot is shorter than 1.2 s.
- **Captions:** "vim" stays although whisper large-v3 and the QA pass hear "them": the idiom is "vim and vigor". "you got to" (not "you've got to") follows large-v3 and the QA pass.
- **Listen once:** I can't hear the audio. Please check the joins at 0:19.5 (into "vim"), 0:40.5 ("You have to have | some intent") and 1:15.4 ("Because | the essence").
