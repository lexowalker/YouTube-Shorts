## 1. Hook title
Too much effort is counterproductive, because you can't see the subtle. (Tao Te Ching, Ch. 1)

## 2. The teaching
Always passionless, thereby observe the subtle; ever intent, thereby observe the apparent. Passion, anger, anxiety and craving hide the subtle, attention shows us what is in front of us, and we need both (Tao Te Ching, Chapter 1, Thomas Cleary translation).

## 5. Notes on choices
- **Teaching:** the user chose this one of three candidates (the others: "A way can be a guide, not a fixed path" and "Names aren't permanent labels", both left for Discussion 2) and approved the transcript before work began.
- **Hook first:** the only reordering. "Too much effort is counterproductive" comes right after his list of what blocks the subtle, and it states the whole teaching in one line.
- **Title card:** "To see the subtle, be still", over the hook.
- **Imagery:** each painting is keyed to its words: mist for "the subtle", a leaping tiger for "vim and vigor", Sesson's storm dragon for anger, anxiety and confusion, a gibbon grasping toward the moon (the Zen image of craving an illusion) for "lust", a falcon for attention and intent, a mountain temple for "that bell", a scholar at his books for "study", a monk quietly at his mending for practice without passion, figures gazing into mist for the quiet that sees, and a vast handscroll for "the whole universe".
- **Screens and crops:** folding-screen crops stay inside one panel. Ma Lin's round fan (*Scholar Reclining and Watching Rising Clouds*) was tried for "observe the subtle nuance", but in 9:16 the scholar fell under the captions and the rest read as empty, so Sōami replaced it. *Night Mooring at Maple Bridge* and Ikkyū's *Listening to the Wind in the Pines* were passed over for the bell because their pale paper would swallow the captions.
- **Head share:** 43%, a little under the usual 50–60%, because the teaching is unusually concrete and imageable; no mode runs longer than 10.1 s.
- **Captions:** "vim" stays although whisper large-v3 and the QA pass hear "them": the idiom is "vim and vigor". "you got to" (not "you've got to") follows large-v3 and the QA pass.
- **Listen once:** I can't hear the audio. Please check the joins at 0:19.9 (into "vim"), 0:40.9 ("You have to have | some intent") and 1:16.2 ("Because | the essence").
