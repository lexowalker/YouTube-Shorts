## 2. The teaching
Heaven and earth are not humane: humaneness is a narrow human construct, and seeing beyond it, to the impermanence of every being and every civilization, is what lets someone see clearly (Tao Te Ching, Chapter 5, Thomas Cleary translation).

## 5. Notes on the choices
- **Why this teaching.** It's the central teaching of Chapter 5 and the one part of the video not yet used (both earlier Shorts are from Chapter 4). It's also the easiest to misread as coldness, so the cut keeps David's own framing: humaneness is a narrow vision, there are things above it, and understanding impermanence "helps someone see clearly".
- **Shape.** The hook is his most quotable line. The Cleary text follows as the thing it explains, and "all beings, not just humans" widens the frame. The straw dog itself comes next (a scarecrow-like figure placed with the dead), then the turn from humaneness to impermanence and depth. The close is the sage line with his gloss, ending on "see clearly".
- **Pictures.** Following your direction, the images go beyond ink scrolls to whatever matches each line, all still real, pre-20th-century works from one CC0 collection. The two tomb dogs are literal straw-dog stand-ins (funerary figures of dogs). No suitable open-access scarecrow painting turned up, so that line stays on the Han dog. Homer's stormy sea was considered for "deeper" and dropped as too violent. The cosmic ocean is gentler and closer to the meaning.
- **Caption accuracy.** medium.en, two large-v3 passes (wide and narrow windows) and the independent QA pass on the finished audio were compared. Corrections are in `short.json` as `word_overrides`, each with its reason. He says "gonna", so the caption says "gonna". "No, this is" is the majority reading of a short phrase one pass heard as "Now, this is interesting". It's in the audio but left off the screen, so nothing uncertain is shown. All 176 caption words match the re-transcription.
- **Framing.** He glances aside 0.8 s into the hook, faster than the smoothed crop can follow, so that segment uses a fixed crop (`crop_fixes`, a new pipeline option).
- **Sound.** The original recording, cut in pauses with 12 ms fades and de-clicked only between words, with one static gain to a −1 dBTP peak (−21.9 LUFS integrated). No music or processing.
- **Thumbnails.** `thumbnails/thumbnail_face.jpg` uses 20:24 of the source (eyes to camera, mouth at rest). `thumbnail_book_cover.jpg` is *The Essential Tao* cover (Amazon's 306×500 scan, so soft at full size).
