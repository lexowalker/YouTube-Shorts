## 1. Hook title
The More You Chase Success, the Less You See

## 2. The teaching
"All beings work, without exception: if they live without possessiveness, act without presumption, and do not dwell on success, then by this very nondwelling success will not leave." Do your work, do your best and keep going. Obsess over success, measured in money and fame, and you can't see clearly; it's like trying to be happy (Tao Te Ching, Chapter 2, Thomas Cleary translation).

## 5. Notes on choices
- **Obsession gets worldly imagery:** Burgkmair's Avarice is blindfolded and clutching her purse, so she literally can't see. Later, the jewelled prince and the gold coin stand for "money and fame".
- **Work:** Liang Kai's villagers at every stage of making silk are the "all beings work", shown as two sections of one scroll.
- **Keep going:** a barefoot ascetic walking on along the river.
- **Peace, harmony, joy:** Zhao Mengfu's quiet river village.
- **Ending:** he delivers "someone who tries to be happy… a guaranteed recipe for being unhappy or miserable" to camera, so it stays on his face.
- **Head share:** 46%. No mode runs over 11.3 s.
- **Listen once:** I can't hear the audio. Please check the hook (0:00–0:08), where two cuts fall under the first image, and the join at 0:31.0 ("and | just keep going").
