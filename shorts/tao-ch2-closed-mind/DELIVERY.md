## 1. Hook title
You Can Be Bigoted on the Right, the Left, or the Center

## 2. The teaching
Cleary's note on Chapter 2: "When it is forgotten that conventional conceptions are conventional conceptions, and they are taken for objective facts that everyone knows and no one questions, then narrow-minded bigotry and blind prejudice can develop unopposed." Bigotry means a closed mind, locked into one way of conceiving things, when our whole way of looking at the world is conditioned by our environment. Anyone who can look more holistically sees more clearly.

## 5. Notes on choices
- **Blind prejudice:** one print in two crops. The leader marching ahead carries "taken for objective facts that everyone knows", and the men tumbling into the ditch arrive on "blind prejudice".
- **A closed mind:** a pair of shut doors, held through "not open… locked into one fixed way".
- **Changing attitudes:** a waterfall, never the same water twice.
- **Modern media:** Instagram, TV and TikTok get a classical stand-in that keeps the meaning, a packed Edo theatre with every eye on the stage.
- **A slice of the world:** a princess looking out of one small window.
- **Ending:** a hermit's retreat looking out over the whole misty valley, for seeing "more holistically".
