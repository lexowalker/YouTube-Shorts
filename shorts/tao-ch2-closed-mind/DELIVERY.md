## 1. Hook title
You Can Be Bigoted on the Right, the Left, or the Center

## 2. The teaching
Cleary's note on Chapter 2: "When it is forgotten that conventional conceptions are conventional conceptions, and they are taken for objective facts that everyone knows and no one questions, then narrow-minded bigotry and blind prejudice can develop unopposed." Bigotry is a closed mind. It can sit on the right, the left or the center, in a person, a nation or a corporation (Boeing, Apple). Our attitudes change over time and are conditioned by everything we see, so whoever can get out of that and look more holistically sees more clearly.

## 5. Notes on choices
- **The user's notes on the transcript:** open on Cleary's note, not the hook, and use Cleary only there; the rest is Dr. Arredondo. The Apple and Boeing examples are in.
- **Order:** "And what we need is something from the outside, some creativity from the outside" plays where he said it, right after "locked into one way of conceiving things". That saves two jump cuts, and it leads straight into "Let's, instead of talking about a nation, let's talk about a corporation."
- **"If a corporation becomes so":** two transcription passes heard "knows that" there and one heard "becomes so", so it is cut rather than guessed. The line now reads "let's talk about a corporation, knows that they're good…", with a natural pause on the join.
- **Imagery:** every image shows what he is saying at that moment:
  - a cosmos everyone "knew" was true, then the blind leading the blind;
  - Daumier's smug deputies for arrogance and bias, and a padlock for the closed mind;
  - Cole's fallen empire for "the nation… starts to degenerate";
  - Flammarion's traveller breaking through the sky for "something from the outside";
  - the 737 MAX, then the grounded fleet, for Boeing;
  - Apple Park and an Apple Store queue for Apple;
  - a "More Doctors Smoke Camels" ad, then a No Smoking sign, for changing attitudes;
  - a 1958 family round the TV, then a phone full of apps, for being conditioned by what we see;
  - Earthrise, the whole Earth seen from outside, for looking more holistically.
- **Left on his face:** the hook, "if you didn't spank your child", and "a tiny slice of the world".
- **Head share:** 50%. No mode runs over 12 s.
